# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Open rooms. A scene can be drawn open as well as boxed: `drawing({ scene, format })` and `<Drawing format>` take `wide` (1600 by 860, for a desktop's first screen) or `tall` (800 by 900, for a phone), drawn without a border, the floor running past both edges and the lamp hung from above the sheet, so the room becomes part of the page's section instead of a picture beside it. A scene offers them through `open` in `defineScene()`; one without them is drawn boxed. `stage()` takes the sheet's size, and `sceneIn()` picks a scene's size and composer for a format. The `room` scene has both.
- `hero-01` is open by default (`room="open"`; `room="boxed"` keeps the old layout): on a wide container the room fills the section behind the text, a screen tall less `--hero-top`, cropped like a photograph; on a narrow one a tall room follows the text, full width. The home page uses it.
- The Listening Room scene is a moment rather than an inventory: someone holds the record they just found high under the lamp, the shopkeeper raises a hand to them, a record plays on the turntable (sound rising from it), and someone at the bin sways with headphones on. Every seed of `room` draws differently from before; the other scenes and every object are unchanged. The visitor gains three poses, `cheer`, `listen` and `hail`, and two objects, `visitor-cheer` and `visitor-listening`; `clothes()` takes `swing`, each arm's angle; `fan()`, `soundLines()` and the headphones join the shared parts (`pen/objects/parts`).
- Seven Everyday drawings redrawn to the Indie set's standard, with their ids, names and tags unchanged (`drawings-everyday`): a broad padlock with its thickness at the side and the keyhole on a small plate; two keys hanging from a split ring, an old one with a ring bow and a newer one with an inked head; a magnifying glass at a slant with an inked handle; the back of a sealed envelope with a wax seal; a paper dart seen from above with a glimpse of its keel, without the dashed trail; a rotary telephone with its dial, handset and a coiled cord; an upright clipboard with three ticked rows and a turned-up corner, without the pencil. Their tiles changed size to keep the same air as the rest of the library, so a fixed-width `<Drawing>` of one of them draws a little larger.
- Renamed the four fonts to Fineliner Naïve Sans, Inline, Inline Fill and Tramline, with matching download files and font metadata; the letters and their seeded takes are unchanged.
- MÅLTID, a second demo at `/maaltid`: the website of a made-up Copenhagen restaurant cooking Italy's peasant food in the north and pouring natural wine. Home, tonight's menu, the wine list as tasting notes, and a booking form (an evening from Wednesday to Saturday, a time, guests, a name and email; full times struck through; the booking confirmed in place). Its look is its own (bond paper, the oxblood pen, a calm hand, typewriter labels), and its drawings (a long table, a cast-iron pot, a crate of wine and its dining room) were made with the drawing skill in the site's own module. `pen/objects/rooms` exports `ROOMS`, the kit's own scene names, so lists and counts can leave out a project's scenes.
- The specimen's lettering shows the three faces the same way: a title at one size, the face's name, what it is and its files beside it, then its letters and figures at one size, with a faint rule between faces; the set they share (marks, accents, languages) closes the section once.
- A favicon drawn by the pen: a capital F on bond paper that turns night paper in a dark browser (`public/favicon.svg`, with a 32 px `favicon.ico` and an iOS touch icon, from `tools/render-favicon.mjs`). The site had none, so every browser asked for `/favicon.ico` and got a 404.
- An appearance panel on every page: the header's Appearance button (its dot in the current pen) opens a sheet from the right with the studio's presets and every setting. The page stays undimmed and scrolls, so a change shows in place, and the choice follows you to every page. Listening Room has it in its own header, and MÅLTID in a demo strip above its own.
- A custom pen: pick Custom in the pen row, start from one of six directions (indigo, teal, moss, ochre, rust, plum) and tune its hue and strength. Each paper sets how dark text must be and how much colour any hue may take, in OKLCH, so every hue passes 4.5:1 by day and turns a pale gel pen at night, with a wash to match. `useAppearance` gains `penHue` and `penStrength`; the studio exports the result. The pen row is its own registry item, `appearance-pen`.
- MÅLTID's appearance panel starts from MÅLTID's own look (also its first preset) and saves under a key of its own, so restyling the restaurant leaves the docs alone and the other way round. `appearanceScript`, `useAppearance`, `readSaved`, `save` and `AppearanceControl` take an optional storage key for this.
- MÅLTID, first round of an overhaul. "Show parts" in the demo strip outlines every kit part on the page and names it, each name a link to its docs. Tonight's menu is a printed card in a doubled drawn border, with "Tonight" lettered, the menu's number, hand notes and vegan badges; "Leave out" (gluten, dairy, fish, nuts) takes dishes off the card, and each dish opens in a dialog with its drawing and its story. A booking ends in a drawn ticket with a booking number and code and a toast; "Change a booking" (a dialog, or a drawer on phones) finds it by its code and cancels it after a confirmation.
- The paper's dust shows on phones. A speck under a pixel across shows on a 1x screen because it is rounded up to a soft pixel, but a phone draws it true to size and it vanishes; screens of 1.5 dppx or more now take flecks held to about a pixel across (`--tex-flecks-sharp`), in the same places. Fibres are drawn larger on phones.
- Scenes no longer wait for the page's script. A drawing with `drawOnView` was sent hidden and shown only by the script, so on a phone a scene sat as a scatter of dots (each stroke's round cap) for up to 2.5 s and then appeared whole. The server's drawing now shows at once; a drawing out of view when the script arrives is hidden there and drawn on when it comes into view, and one drawn in the browser (an empty state) still draws on. So a hero in view on load no longer draws itself on. The site preloads Newsreader, so the text no longer reflows when the font arrives a beat after the first paint.
- Touch. A long press on a control shows its tooltip without pressing it, as on Android, and it goes 1.5 s after the finger lifts; a tap opens and closes a term's definition, which a phone could not reach before. With line boil on hover, a tap on a drawing boils it once and it settles again. Android's grey tap box is gone; controls show their own press.
- Drawings no longer blink while they load. Line boil cycled whatever takes a drawing had, each for a third of the cycle, so a scene with only the server's take blanked out two thirds of the time under the pointer (or everywhere with boil always on) until the browser had drawn the other two, and a drawing that never gets more takes, like the drawing skill's example, blinked for as long as it was hovered. Takes now cycle only once all three are there.

A review round after 0.1.0, from the maintainer's notes on the live site and a full design review.

- Paper: bond is the default stock. The paper's low-frequency clouding (mottle and relief) is at a fifth of its old strength, so it reads as paper rather than grease or marble; the flecks and fibres stay. The paper lies on the root, so it fills the window on short pages, and the scrollbar gutter is stable. Wells and hovers (`--tint`) are the paper's own darker tone instead of a grey.
- Drawings: armchair, record crate, phone, keyboard and mouse, teapot, moka pot, chair, window, cassette, camera, desk, cactus and cat redrawn or repaired to the object sheet's standard; `visitor` stands empty-handed (`visitor-record` holds the sleeve); every figure in a scene is only their clothes. The pen starts a closed shape mid-edge (no spike at the seam) and centres its wobble; scene masks are named per scene and hand. A sized drawing (`h-28 [&>svg]:h-full`) fits its height.
- Drawings, second pass: every object tidied to the standard of yesterday's redraws without leaving the naive line. Closed outlines are one stroke, so corners meet; inner lines end on the outline or under a part drawn over them instead of poking through. `box()` and `pad()` draw their silhouette in one stroke, and `shapes.ts` gains `banded()`, `drum()` and `sprig()`. New detail where an object was bare: the turntable's lid and tonearm, the record's grooves, the sphere of the disco ball, the desk lamp's sprung arm, the headphones' yokes, the sunflower's petals and seeds, rooftops and leaves in the window.
- People: younger and dressed for everyday. Loose sweaters, hoodies, open overshirts and tees over wide trousers with rolled hems or a short skirt, and trainers (`objects/clothes.ts`); hair is a pocket of ink in eight styles (bob, crop, fringe, bun, curls, long, ponytail, waves) instead of outlines that read as grey; larger soft-square glasses. Visitors wear the same clothes. New: `visitor-seated` and `person-seated`, someone at work seen from behind on an office chair, and a fourth scene, `work`, at a desk under a window.
- Fineliner Naïve Inline and Inline Fill: the same skeleton as a contrasted serif. A broad nib's rule makes verticals and \ diagonals thick (two lines, the tint between) and horizontals and / diagonals thin (one line), with serifs on the baseline and cap line and ticks on arm ends. Two layered fonts, so the tint takes the page's wash: `<Lettering face="inline">` sets the outlines and lays the Fill under them in CSS. 121 KB and 42 KB as WOFF2, opt-in through the `font-inline` registry item; the site loads them from `/fonts/` only where used. The build keeps a copy of each compressed font, so the item embeds the same Inline as `/fonts/` (it had embedded a damaged one). Checked in Chrome and Firefox. Every font's line metrics are now pinned (USE_TYPO_METRICS), so a glyph's bounds can no longer move the baseline on Windows.
- Our own font: Fineliner Naïve Sans, the pen as a typeface (MIT). Round-ended monoline capitals from the skeleton alphabet, every Latin-script European language (197 glyphs: Æ Ø Å, Œ Þ Ð Ŋ ẞ and every accented capital, figures, punctuation, symbols), lowercase typing the same capitals, three takes per glyph rotating through `calt`, kerning; 73 KB as WOFF2. Built by `npm run build:fonts` (`tools/fonts`, deterministic) into `public/fonts/` and embedded in the kit's `styles/line-sans.generated.css`, with a `font-line` utility. `<Lettering>` sets it as real text by default (`face="line"`), so wordmarks, headings, room headers, label cards and record sleeves are searchable text; the share images set it as outlines. Liner, Stilt and Ribbon are removed.
- Fineliner Naïve Tramline: the drawn Tramline as a real font. Every stroke is three parallel lines, each wobbled on its own and running a little past its ends or stopping short, so they cross where strokes meet, and each take tilts and lifts its letter; the same 197 glyphs and three takes as the other faces, 243 KB as WOFF2, opt-in through the `font-tramline` registry item. `<Lettering face="tramline">` sets it as text, so `<Lettering>` is now always text (its `seed`, `hand` and `still` props are gone); `lettering()` in the pen engine still draws Tramline as SVG from a seed, which the desk's Letters mode uses. Room headers, label cards, record sleeves and hero-01 set a Tramline title as one line of text instead of a drawing per word.
- Speed. Drawings are a sixth of the bytes: a layer's mask now holds only the blanks in front of it that reach it (every later blank before, so masks grew with the square of a scene's layers), and paths are written as relative steps; the same pixels in every object and scene. The home page went from 3.9 MB of HTML to 915 KB and the specimen from 8 MB to 1.8 MB. A hydrating `<Drawing>` keeps the server's markup instead of drawing its takes again, and draw-on measures every stroke before animating one take, so the home page's scene no longer stalls and restarts on first load (blocking time at 4× CPU from 1.6 s to about 0.4 s). The paper's filtered grain lies on a fixed layer of its own and the specks on the body, so a collapsible opening or anything else that moves no longer repaints the paper (frames of 100 ms before, none over 17 ms now), with the paper looking as it did. The site serves Naïve Sans as a preloaded file, so its render-blocking stylesheet is 51 KB gzipped instead of 126 KB.
- A listening room of our own. The `room` scene followed the reference label's composition too closely (a door, a disco ball, a treehouse picture, a turntable between speakers on a record shelf, a sunflower), so it is now the shop floor at Listening Room, seen straight on: records face out on wall rails, a gig poster and a pendant lamp, browser bins with someone flicking through one, and the counter with its till and a turntable, often with someone behind it. Five new Indie objects make it (record bin, sleeve rail, counter, till, gig poster), the visitor gains a `browse` pose, and the pinned picture's treehouse is now a sailboat; every other drawing is unchanged. Everything that shows the `room` scene (hero, specimen, sign-in, article, room header, share images) shows the shop. The gpt-image explorations generated from the label photograph are gone from the repository; the README shows the kit's own four scenes (`tools/render-scenes.mjs`), and the label-card demo and the specimen's Tramline sample no longer borrow the label's words.
- One take per drawing in the page. The server sends a drawing's first take only, and the browser draws the other two where line boil can show them: in idle time for drawings in view, one take at a time, and at once under the pointer; with boil off, under reduced motion or on a phone without hover, never. Hover boil stays the default. Pages halve again (home 120 KB to 55 KB of HTML gzipped, the specimen 238 KB to 103 KB, the library 198 KB to 75 KB). `<Drawing>` does it by itself; a page whose drawings render without React in the browser calls `boilTakesIn()` from `drawing/drawing-takes` once. `drawing()` takes `from` to draw only the later takes.
- Drawings: every drawing on /drawings opens at the desk with the same seed. The desk's count no longer says "1 faces".
- The drawing skill: `skills/fineliner-drawings`, also the registry item `drawing-skill` (installs into `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills`), teaches a coding agent to draw new objects, people and scenes in the kit's style from the project's own module, and to check them in a contact sheet (`scripts/render.mjs`). The pen engine opens for it: any collection name, `defineScene()` and `stage()`, and the shapes and people parts exported. Tested on three prompts with and without the skill in a fresh app (`skills/fineliner-drawings/evals`); `test:consumer` installs it and type-checks its example. Its tile check found 16 kit objects running to or past their tile's edge at some seed (the waving hand by 13 px, the record a visitor holds by 13 px); their tiles now leave at least 8 px, except the hanging disco ball and the portrait's shoulders.
- 23 new drawings, 85 in all. A fifth collection, **Everyday**, gives the moments every site has something to draw: an open door (welcome, sign-up), an empty basket, an hourglass, a padlock, keys on a ring, a magnifying glass (search, no results), a sealed envelope, a parcel tied with string, a paper plane (sent), a rotary telephone and a ticked checklist. Food & drink gains a kitchen for simple food and natural wine: an oil tin, a swing-top jar of pickles, a cheese board, a rolling pin across a sheet of pasta, a corkscrew in its cork, a pavement sign, tomatoes on the vine, basil, porcini, carrots, a mackerel and an oyster with lemon. Chosen from an [idea bank](docs/ideas.md) that sorts two brainstorms against the style.
- Drawings install by collection. The `pen` item is the engine alone and registers no drawings; each collection is its own item (`drawings-indie`, `drawings-food`, `drawings-studio`, `drawings-people`, `drawings-everyday`, or `drawings-all`) and registers when its module is imported (`@/fineliner/pen/objects/indie`), and the kit's four rooms are the `scenes` item (`@/fineliner/pen/objects/rooms`). A project installs and ships only the drawings it shows. **Breaking:** a project that draws library objects imports their collections; the people parts (`clothes`, `head`, `HAIR`…) move to `@/fineliner/pen/objects/people`. `boilTakesIn()` takes a loader for a static page's collections. /drawings shows six drawings per collection, and each collection has a page with all of it and its install command.
- The drawing skill has a page, `/drawings/skill`: its worked example drawn, the install command, what to ask for, what the agent does, and the skill with its references to read. Linked from /drawings and the home page. Its contact-sheet script no longer writes a cache into `node_modules`, so it runs in a sandboxed agent with a linked `node_modules`.
- Kit: pen markup gets instance-unique SVG ids (`useOwnIds`), so a hidden copy of the same drawing no longer breaks another's masks (a record sleeve's disc covered its cover). A record title too long to letter readably is set in small capitals. The chart tooltip follows the pointer at once instead of gliding for 400 ms. Settings-01 has one label column and `bare` and `appearance` props; pricing-01's "Most chosen" stamp sits with its plan.
- Site: the specimen is one composed sheet; the components index shows a preview of every part; docs pages share one header, a component nav and previous/next, with even demo rows and one copy affordance; block previews take their block's full height; the studio's panel no longer clips; the desk puts its controls under the drawing on phones; /drawings separates its collections and aligns its labels. Listening Room shares one page frame, Maya is staff throughout, sessions stack on phones, the overview shows every day, and badges count what the pages show. The home page's hold demo is one card with Jonas's request.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-02)

The first release: shadcn parity drawn with one pen, Fineliner's own extras and blocks, the pen engine and its drawings, the theme studio and Listening Room.

Phase 5: the release.

- Share images drawn by the pen at build time, one per main page, block and component, with Open Graph and Twitter tags (`SITE_URL` makes them absolute).
- `test:consumer` installs into a Next.js 16 App Router app as well as Vite, and fails if a kit file a server component imports uses hooks without `"use client"`.
- Accessibility: drawings with `title=""` are hidden from assistive tech (they were named three times); calendar days are named by weekday and date; the combobox chevron, the sidebar trigger's state, command and item separators and the sign-in block's autocomplete fixed; kraft's quiet pen reaches 4.5:1. See `docs/accessibility-review.md`.
- Performance: a docs page loads only its own demo (129 KB of JavaScript gzipped instead of 566 KB). See `docs/quality.md`.
- The site and registry are live at https://naiveindieline.com (Dokploy, rebuilt on push to `main`).
- Docs: the README for installing and using the kit, `docs/quality.md` and `docs/accessibility-review.md`.

Phase 4: extras, blocks, the theme studio and the home page.

- Twelve extras, each its own registry item with a docs page: `label-card`, `record-sleeve`, `track-list`, `tasting-note`, `price-tag`, `hand-note`, `edition-number`, `drawn-border`, `room-header`, `pull-quote`, `reading-progress` and `appearance-control`.
- Seven blocks (`registry:block`): `sign-in-01`, `settings-01`, `dashboard-01`, `pricing-01`, `article-01`, `empty-01` and `hero-01`, with live previews at `/docs/blocks`. The `fineliner` umbrella leaves blocks out.
- The theme studio at `/studio`: presets, every setting on live previews, and the export as CSS or a shadcn `registry:theme` item.
- The home page: the hero block, the drawing library, components in use, an A–Z index and install; the specimen moved to `/specimen`.
- `drawing`: a caller's `ref` on `Drawing` is merged with the one draw-on needs; the takes' grid sits in the components layer, so `hidden` hides a drawing.
- Listening Room's sign-in and settings are the blocks.
- `test:consumer` also installs `record-sleeve` and `sign-in-01`.

Phase 3: the rest of shadcn parity.

- 38 more parts, each its own registry item with a docs page: table, pagination, breadcrumb, accordion, collapsible, command, combobox, sheet, drawer, menubar, navigation menu, context menu, hover card, calendar, sonner, sidebar, scroll area, resizable, carousel, input OTP, input group, button group, empty, item, aspect ratio, typography, direction, and the conversation set (message, bubble, message scroller, marker, trace, attachment, code block, questionnaire). Tables rule their rows with hand-drawn lines cheap enough for 200 rows; sheets, drawers and toasts fade and grow, never slide; code is highlighted with one pen.

- `pen`: `plot()` for strokes through data, and the straight `next` mark.
- `base`: `shadowRoom` collision padding; `frameStyle` reads words through nested elements; drawings carry `fl-size-own`, so shadcn's icon size rule leaves pen marks alone; the typewriter's italic.
- Listening Room: a sidebar shell, a page per record, sessions with a booking sheet, and an overview.
- `test:consumer` also installs `table` and `chart`.

Phase 2: core primitives and Listening Room.

- 28 shadcn primitives on Base UI, each its own registry item with shadcn's names, props, variants and `data-slot`s: button, badge, card, input, textarea, label, field, checkbox, radio group, switch, select, native select, tabs, toggle, toggle group, separator, skeleton, avatar, alert, progress, slider, kbd, tooltip, popover, dialog, alert dialog, dropdown menu and spinner. Fields keep a steady pen line; buttons, boxes and sheets are drawn frames seeded by their own words; the main action and chosen options are pockets of ink; ticks, dots and underlines are pen marks put down; menus, selects, popovers and dialogs float on paper with the cartoonist's shadow and grow from their trigger. Avatar fallbacks are portraits drawn from the name.

- Listening Room, the demo product: a record shop's catalogue, sign-in and settings at `/listening-room`.

Phase 1: the pen and the drawings.

- `pen` (new item): the framework-free pen engine. `drawing({ object | scene, seed, hand, takes })` and `lettering(text, { face, seed, hand, takes })` return one SVG per take; `penMark()` draws ticks, rings, underlines, strikes, arrows, chevrons and pills; `define()` with `objectsIn()` adds objects. 55 objects in four collections (indie, food and drink, studio, people), three scenes (listening room, studio, bistro) and four lettering faces (Liner, Stilt, Ribbon, Tramline). The same seed draws the same SVG in Node and in Chromium.

- Registry: `npm run registry`, `registry:check` and `test:consumer` (installs `drawing` into a fresh Vite app offline and builds it).
- Site: `/drawings`, the library by collection; `/desk`, the drawing desk; the specimen's hero scene; a lettered wordmark.
